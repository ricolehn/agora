Privacy Policy

Agora – web app and Android app (org.agora.app)

1. Who is responsible?

Agora is software that churches and associations run on their own server. The controller responsible for processing your data is the operator of the Agora instance you are registered with – that is, the church or association that gave you access. If you signed up for an event as a guest, it is the church or association whose event it is. The Android app itself stores no data with the app provider; it only connects to the server whose address you enter.

2. What data is processed

  • Account: name, e-mail address, password (only as a hash), groups and permissions, settings, optionally a profile picture.
  • Membership fees and finances: fee status, booked payments, standing orders, donations, expenses with receipts and your requests about them.
  • Appointments and events: registrations, waiting lists and duties in the duty roster.
  • Guests without an account: if you sign up for an event through a link its organisers shared: your full name, e-mail address and age, and whether you have a place or are on the waiting list. The organisers of the event see these details; members see your name in the list of participants. Your browser remembers your registration on this device.
  • Mentoring: confidential messages between you and your mentor; stored encrypted on the server.
  • AI support (if enabled by the operator): your questions and the answers; answers are generated by an AI.
  • Media (if enabled by the operator): files you add to the media library; they are stored in the operator's Nextcloud, and the server log notes who added them.
  • Technical: tokens for push notifications, server logs (e.g. IP address) for security.

3. Purposes

The data is used exclusively for the functions of the app: member management and fees, appointments and duty planning, mentoring, AI support and notifications. There is no advertising, no tracking and no sale of data.

4. Sharing with service providers

  • Push notifications: in the Android app via Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google), in the browser via your browser's push service. A device token as well as the title and text of the notification are transmitted.
  • AI support: only if the operator has enabled it, your questions are sent to the AI provider chosen by the operator in order to generate an answer.
  • Media: only if the operator has enabled it, the media library is a folder in the operator's Nextcloud; pictures and files are shown and added through it.
  • E-mail: e-mail notifications are sent through the operator's mail server.

All connections to the server are encrypted via HTTPS.

5. Retention period

  • Your data is stored for as long as your account exists.
  • Decided finance requests are deleted automatically 30 days after the decision.
  • Guests' details are deleted when they cancel or are removed, and automatically 30 days after the event.
  • Booked payments are kept for accounting reasons even after an account has been deleted.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. You can delete your account yourself at any time – in the app, in the browser or under Delete account. As a guest you cancel your registration with the link in your confirmation e-mail or on the event's page. For all other matters, please contact the administrators of your church or association.

7. Reporting content

AI answers and mentoring conversations can be reported in the app; conversation partners can be blocked. Reports go to the operator's administrators.
