# Delete your Agora account

**Agora** – web app and Android app (`org.agora.app`)

Agora runs on the server of your church or association, so your account and your data live there. You can delete your account yourself at any time, in one of three ways:

## How to delete your account

1. **In the Android app:** Settings → “Delete account” → enter your password → confirm.
2. **In the browser:** sign in to your Agora server → Settings → “Delete account”.
3. **Without the app:** open the deletion page of your Agora server – the address of your server followed by `/account-deletion` (for example `https://agora.example.org/account-deletion`) – and enter your e-mail address and password.

Forgot your password or have questions? Contact the administrators of your church or association – they can delete your account as well.

## What is deleted

- Your access (login, e-mail address, name, settings) and your profile picture
- Your registrations for appointments; your duties are released again
- Your mentoring conversations including their messages and your mentor profile
- Your finance requests as well as device tokens for push notifications

## What is kept

Payments that have already been booked (amount, date, name) stay with the association or church, because they have to be kept for accounting by law. All other information about you in the member list is removed. The deletion happens immediately.
