With port and path if your Nextcloud needs them, e.g. https://cloud.example.org:8443/nextcloud. The address of the Files app works too.

Folder rights

A right applies to this folder and every folder in it, until a folder below sets its own. Read: open and download. Write: also upload and create folders. No access: does not see the folder. The most specific right counts: a person's goes before their groups', a group's before the one for all members (someone in several groups gets the most of these). Whoever has no access to the base folder does not see the media library at all. Nothing can be deleted.
